Support

Tessera asks for access to a folder. Why?

The App Store version runs in Apple's sandbox and can only read what you allow. Grant the startup disk once and the whole disk can be mapped from then on; the grant is remembered.

The status bar says some folders could not be read.

macOS protects folders such as Mail, Messages, Safari data and other users' homes. Give Tessera Full Disk Access in System Settings › Privacy & Security, then rescan.

The scan total is smaller than what Finder says is used.

Select the Hidden space tile or the scan summary in the inspector. The gap is explained there: purgeable space, local Time Machine snapshots, other users' folders, folders without access, and other volumes in the same APFS container.

Does Tessera delete files permanently?

No. Everything goes to the Trash. Empty the Trash to reclaim the space. Local snapshots can hold on to deleted files for up to 24 hours.

Something else?

Open an issue on GitHub with the macOS version and what you scanned.
