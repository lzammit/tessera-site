Tessera Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 September 2026

Tessera is a disk usage map for macOS. It runs entirely on your Mac.

What Tessera collects

Nothing. The app has no account, no analytics, no crash reporting of its own, and makes no network requests. It does not read the contents of your files; it reads only names, sizes and dates to draw the map.

What stays on your Mac

Your recent scan locations, display preferences and the folder grants you give through the open panel are stored in the app's own preferences on your Mac. Saved scans are files you choose where to put. Removing the app removes all of it.

Permissions

Tessera reads only the folders you grant it. macOS may additionally ask for Full Disk Access to show protected folders such as Mail or Messages. That permission is managed in System Settings and can be revoked at any time.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.
